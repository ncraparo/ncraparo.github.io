Wayne Philip Craparo is a former American Porsche Owners Club Cup Series Driver & Crew Member.[2]

Porsche Owners Club Racing

[edit]

Wayne Craparo, was active from 1995 to 1999, and during those years he competed in 4 seasons of the Porsche Owners Club's Cup Racing Series.[3][4] He raced and competed in the R11/CI Class, driving the #49 Porsche 914.[5][2] He was also on the crew for the #91 Porsche 911 team sponsored by Valvoline Instant Oil Change[6] (formerly known as: EZ Lube - Fast Oil Change Experts).[7]

Post Racing Life

[edit]

Following his racing career, Wayne settled down with his family in Palos Verdes Estates, Los Angeles;[1] where he became a marketing executive[8][9] and later operated the marketing firm, Pacific Marketing.[10]
